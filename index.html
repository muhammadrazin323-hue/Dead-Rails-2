DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • WASD = Move Character
  • SHIFT = Sprint (uses Stamina)
  • SPACE = Jump / Climb Train
  • CTRL / C = Crouch (reduces recoil)
  • Mouse = Rotate Camera (Click viewport to lock)
  • Left Click = Shoot / Melee Attack
  • Right Click = Aim Down Sights (Zoom)
  • R = Reload Weapon
  • E = Interact (Loot / Throttle / Refuel / Repair)
  • 1 – 5 = Switch Weapons
  • TAB = Open Saddlebag Inventory
  • B = Open Trading Post / Express Shop
  • F / H / L / V = Throttle / Whistle / Lamp / Camera
  • ESC = Pause / Menu

SURVIVAL HANDBOOK

Train Operation: Step onto the locomotive cab and press E on the Throttle Lever (or press F) to start or stop the train. Stopping near towns lets you loot safely without leaving the train behind.

Fuel & Repairs: Coal Sacks and Oil Barrels add fuel when you interact with the glowing Firebox (E) or click Stoke All Fuel in your inventory. Use Scrap Metal to weld hull damage.

Broken Tracks & Bosses: Watch the railway ahead! If bandits or fallen rocks barricade the rails, stop the train and clear the barricade (E) before colliding!
